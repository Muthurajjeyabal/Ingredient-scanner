Privacy Policy — Insider

Last updated: August 2026

Insider ("the app", "we") is developed by JMR Apps. This policy explains what information the app accesses and how it's used.

What the app accesses

  • Camera / photo library — used only to capture a photo of a product label when you choose to scan something. Photos are sent to an AI service to read the label text and are not stored by us afterward.
  • Local device storage — your scan history and language preference are saved only on your own device (browser local storage). This data is never transmitted to us and is not accessible to anyone else.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect your name, email, phone number, or any account information — the app doesn't require sign-in.
  • We don't track your location.
  • We don't sell or share any data with third parties for advertising.
  • We don't display ads.

Third-party services used

When you scan a label, the photo is sent to Google's Gemini AI API solely to extract ingredient and nutrition text from the image. This is processed per Google's own privacy terms and is not linked to any personal identity, since the app has no account system.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, since it has no account system and no personal data collection.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact JMR Apps.
