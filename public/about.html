About Insider

How it works, and how accurate you can expect it to be — by JMR Apps

What Insider does

Insider reads the back-of-pack label on food, cosmetic, and personal-care products using AI, and turns it into a plain breakdown of ingredients, chemicals, allergens, and nutrition — so you don't have to decode confusing labels yourself.

How the scan works

When you photograph a label, the image is sent to an AI vision model (Google Gemini) that reads the printed text and structures it into the categories you see in the app — ingredients, additives, allergens, and nutrition facts. Nothing is looked up from an external product database for scanned items; everything comes directly from what's printed on your specific pack.

⚠️ AI can make mistakes

Like any AI, it can occasionally misread blurry text, guess at a partially visible word, or miss a line entirely — especially with poor lighting, glare, or small print. The app shows a "partially readable" warning when it isn't confident, but it won't always catch every error.

✅ What we do to reduce errors

  • The AI is instructed to only report what's actually printed on the label — never to invent ingredients, allergens, or numbers that aren't there.
  • A confidence flag is shown when part of a label wasn't fully clear.
  • Marketing claim checks are based only on the label's own ingredient/nutrition data, not outside assumptions.

Please always double-check

For anything that matters to your health — allergies, medical dietary restrictions, or medication interactions — always verify against the physical label yourself, or ask a pharmacist/doctor. Insider is a reading aid, not a certified food-safety or medical tool.

Not medical advice

The "safety" indicators, ingredient notes, and consumption suggestions in the app are general consumer-awareness information based on commonly-discussed additives and nutrition thresholds — not a medical assessment of you personally.

Your data

Scan history is stored only on your own device. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

Found something wrong?

If a scan result looks incorrect, please use the "Report incorrect result" option on that result in the app — it helps us keep improving accuracy.
